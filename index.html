3. Saw Palmetto Berry Extract

Saw palmetto is derived from the berries of a small palm tree and is commonly included in men's wellness supplements. Scientific research on saw palmetto has produced mixed findings for specific prostate and urinary outcomes. 4. Nettle Leaf Extract

Nettle leaf has a long history of traditional use and contains plant compounds with antioxidant properties. It is included in various herbal preparations and men's wellness formulas to complement other botanical ingredients. VigorPeak lists 20 mg of Nettle Leaf Extract per serving. Although nettle has been investigated for several health-related applications, research findings depend on the plant part, extract, and intended use. There is not enough evidence to establish that the specific nettle leaf extract in VigorPeak improves testosterone levels, libido, or male sexual performance.


5. Wild Yam Root Extract

Wild yam is a plant traditionally used in herbal preparations. It contains diosgenin, a naturally occurring compound used in certain laboratory processes to produce steroid compounds. However, the human body does not automatically convert diosgenin into testosterone or other human hormones. VigorPeak lists 20 mg of Wild Yam Root Extract per serving. Despite its traditional use, reliable clinical evidence supporting specific male vitality or sexual performance benefits from oral wild yam supplements remains limited.


6. Sarsaparilla Root Extract

Sarsaparilla is a traditional botanical ingredient used in herbal products and wellness formulations. Its roots contain plant compounds called saponins, which have been investigated for various biological properties. VigorPeak includes 20 mg of Sarsaparilla Root Extract per serving. Although the ingredient has a history of traditional use, strong clinical evidence supporting specific benefits for male vitality, testosterone, libido, or sexual performance is limited. Its inclusion should therefore be viewed as part of the product's botanical blend rather than proof of a particular health outcome.


7. Boron Amino Acid Chelate

Boron is a trace element found in foods and involved in several biological processes. VigorPeak lists 20 mg of Boron Amino Acid Chelate standardized to 5% per serving, according to the supplied product information. Boron has attracted scientific interest for its possible relationship with bone health, mineral metabolism, and hormone-related measures. However, evidence supporting meaningful testosterone-related effects remains limited, and the exact amount of elemental boron provided depends on the product's formulation and labeling. The ingredient should not be considered proof that VigorPeak raises testosterone.
